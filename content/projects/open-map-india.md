# 🇮🇳 OpenMapIndia

**100% GitHub-Native Geocompute, Rural Routing & Infrastructure Intelligence for India.** Covers every State, District, Village, Primary Health Centre (PHC/CHC), and Highway corridor with zero backend, zero API keys, and zero cloud hosting fees.

- **Interactive Live Web App:** [https://maniratansingh.github.io/open-map-india/](https://maniratansingh.github.io/open-map-india/)
- **Repository:** [https://github.com/maniratansingh/open-map-india](https://github.com/maniratansingh/open-map-india)
- **Scope:** 35 States & UTs, 594 Districts, 373,988 Indian Villages.

***

## Highlights & Capabilities

### 1. 100% Zero-External API Architecture (Git-Native & Bulletproof)
- **Zero External Routing APIs:** Does not proxy or depend on third-party routing services (like OSRM demo servers or Google Directions). All rural health vectors and highway connections are precomputed offline and served statically from GitHub.
- **Zero External Basemap Dependencies:** Vector maps render locally via Canvas and WebGL, avoiding external tile rate-limits or quota shutdowns.
- **Zero Database Hosting:** Massive nationwide geographic index stored in structured, byte-range queryable static arrays.

***

### 2. Country-Wide Rural Healthcare Accessibility Mapping
- Direct spatial vector mapping linking any village in India to its nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC), Community Health Centre (CHC), or District Hospital.
- District-level accessibility scoring that quantifies real-world road connectivity and transit time to emergency care.

***

### 3. High-Performance Interactive Browser Engine
- Instant search across 373,988 villages with sub-50ms fuzzy filtering.
- Visual vector overlays rendering arterial national and state highways with topographic elevation profiles.
